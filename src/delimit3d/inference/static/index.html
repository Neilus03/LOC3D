A few things to know
Scene files. Use NPZ with points (N × 3) and colors, or PLY with x/y/z and red/green/blue. RGBN6 models additionally require normals in NPZ or nx/ny/nz in PLY. Model-specific inputs and checkpoint availability are checked by the server.
Moving around. Drag to rotate; right-drag or Shift + drag to pan; scroll to zoom. Press F while the viewer is focused to fit the scene. Clicks select the nearest visible point within the cursor radius.
Interactive models. Select an object, add positive clicks, and use background clicks for exclusions. Run again after changing prompts. Alt + click temporarily adds a background prompt.
Results. The viewer may display a sample of the scene. Prompts refer to original point indices and downloaded predictions cover the full input. Colors identify instance masks; they do not assign semantic classes. The server keeps the two most recently loaded scenes in memory. Load an older scene again if it expires.
